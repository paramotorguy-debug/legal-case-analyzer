Legal Case Analyzer

Route every case to the firm most likely to win it.

An AI agent that learns your network's referral patterns and matches incoming cases to the right firm — with a visible rationale, decline-reason capture, and outcome tracking. Built for personal injury referral economics.

The Problem

Every firm in your network has a wheelhouse — case types, jurisdictions, value tiers, signals they want and don't want. Today that knowledge lives in spreadsheets, intake notes, and someone's memory.

The cost: cases declined that should have been accepted, cases accepted that go nowhere, and referral fees walking out the door on every mismatch.

The Solution

A platform that watches every routing decision your network makes — accepts, declines, overrides, outcomes — and gets sharper at recommending the right firm for the next case.

No black box. Every suggestion comes with a rationale and a confidence level. The user always decides; the system always learns.

How it works

01 — INTAKE

Cases captured with structured signals

Type, value tier, jurisdiction, intake strength, and signals like "hospitalized," "corporate defendant," "surgery likely" — plus free-text notes.

02 — MATCH

AI recommends a firm with rationale

Reads the case, every firm's profile, and the network's full referral history. Returns a single recommendation, the reasons behind it, and a confidence rating.

03 — LEARN

Every decision becomes training data

Accepts, declines (with reasons), overrides, and final outcomes feed back into the model. The system gets sharper on your network's actual patterns — not generic priors.

What's different

  • Decision-capture loop. Every referral teaches the system. No opaque AI — every suggestion has a "why" your team can argue with.
  • Outcome tracking. Settlements, referral fees collected, time-to-close. Know which firms actually convert, not just which accept.
  • Built for the referral economy. Percentage fees, declines, overrides, jurisdictions — all first-class concepts, not bolted on.

The math

Industry estimates put PI intake at 90–120 minutes per case, with another 20–40 minutes spent on routing — deciding which firm gets it, calling around, and re-routing after declines. For a network running 50 cases/month, that's 50–100 hours of pure routing labor before a single case is even worked.
